# Customer pilot preparation

NEXT-009 working proposal. Garrett has not selected the final offer or prices.

## Product direction

One maintenance workspace for companies managing marine/heavy/land equipment.
Company purpose enables own-equipment work, customer work or both; it is not a
separate account or an automatic price tier. Work orders connect the equipment,
calendar, pinned checklists, parts/labour, evidence, review and history. Provider
customer billing retains its own permission and publication rules.

Keep routine actions direct and preserve Field Notes. Prioritize reliability,
clear completion and easy adoption by the entire crew. Use the existing feature
set in a small assisted pilot before selecting more features. Target customer
segment, launch geography and Android distribution remain Garrett's decisions.

## Assisted onboarding session

1. Confirm company purpose, owner, authorized supervisors/mechanics/operators
   and intended customer access. Confirm the actual supported phones/languages.
2. Create the company through normal onboarding; invite selected people through
   the enabled, tested delivery channel. Do not use demo-account access.
3. Import a small reviewed equipment sample. Confirm types, component hierarchy,
   meter units/readings and service baselines before importing the rest.
4. Publish one procedure, attach it to planned work and schedule/assign the job.
5. Have a mechanic execute it, add evidence and submit; a reviewer returns one
   item, then approves the correction. Verify equipment history and next due work.
6. Exercise one operator fault and show its linked corrective work/verification.
7. Demonstrate supported offline behavior, restart/reconnect and one upload;
   explain which actions need a connection and how to find unsent work.
8. If customer work is in the offer, prove approved publication and permitted
   invoice access with the selected regional amounts/rules.
9. Give the company the support contact, data/export process and acceptance form.

## Pilot success receipt

Record customer/company, release version, device/OS, dates and named approver.
Capture: first-job completion without database intervention; recurrence advances
once; no lost/duplicated evidence; correct customer/other-company visibility;
notification receipt; onboarding/support minutes; active users and storage growth;
and whether the buyer accepts the proposed renewal price. Mark untested steps
explicitly. Start billing only under the selected pilot agreement.

Customer sign-off belongs to the customer/Garrett. Automated tests and a demo
journey are supporting evidence, not their acceptance.
