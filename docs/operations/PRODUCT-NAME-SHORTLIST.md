# Product name shortlist — September 14, 2026

Proposals for Garrett; no application rename or domain purchase authorized yet.

1. **Rivetrail** — recommended. Rivet suggests practical mechanical work; trail
   suggests equipment history, inspections and a traceable repair record. Fits
   heavy equipment and marine customers without tying the product to one region.
   Suggested line: "Keep equipment working. Keep the history."
2. **Workkeel** — clear connection to keeping work steady and organized, with a
   marine influence. Less direct for customers focused entirely on land equipment.
3. **Fieldkeel** — combines field service with the idea of a reliable foundation;
   marine influence is stronger than Rivetrail.

Preliminary exact-name web searches found no obvious maintenance-software result
for these three spellings. This is a small naming screen, not a claim of legal
availability. IronLedger was discarded because active software already uses it:
https://www.ironledger.ai/ and https://www.ironledger.app/.
Domain selection is expressly deferred until Garrett is home.

Direction: a practical equipment-maintenance workspace for owners and service
teams, covering inspection, work, evidence and service history. Keep the name
broad enough for both customer equipment and an organization's own equipment.
