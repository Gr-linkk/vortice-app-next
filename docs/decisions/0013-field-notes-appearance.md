# 0013 — Field Notes appearance

Accepted 2026-09-07 by Garrett after rendered UI audit and concept review.

Use warm ivory, deep teal, restrained orange accents and fine technical equipment
drawings. Apply the same hierarchy in dark mode. This supersedes PROJECT.md's
instruction to preserve the previous navy visual language.

Home prioritizes current work and attention over tools. Planning opens with the
schedule; unscheduled work, attention and service plans remain reachable without
competing with it. Use work-order vocabulary consistently, preserving separate
company and provider workflows and all existing role/capability boundaries.

Settings offers System, Light and Dark. Save the preference on this device and
restore it before the first app frame. System follows platform brightness.
Semantic colors come from the nearest Theme, including dialogs and status chips.

Equipment illustrations are bundled, offline category drawings, not photographs
or claims about an asset's exact model. Resolve full known type IDs or catalog
names; unknown types and failed image loads get a neutral equipment fallback.
Do not infer a category from a coincidental UUID suffix.

Preserve existing saves, evidence, authorization and completion rules. Visual
verification, automated tests, APK packaging and physical-device acceptance are
separate evidence. Scope and results: NOW-017.
