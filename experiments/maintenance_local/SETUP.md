# Connect your inspection phone

Keep **Vortice Local Maintenance** open on the office computer and phone. Start
by creating the company, equipment and checklist on the office, then prepare an
inspection. Your hosted Vortice app and the earlier lab remain separate.

1. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot.
2. On the office choose **Connect or replace a phone**. Check the computer's
   Wi-Fi address. The list refreshes when the screen opens, when the app returns
   and while the screen is open. **Refresh addresses** checks again. If multiple
   addresses appear, ask the person managing the computer which belongs to this
   network.
3. Choose **Create phone invitation**, then **Copy invitation**. Copying happens
   only on the computer. Bring the complete private text to the phone using a
   transfer method you already trust, open it there and copy all the text.
4. On the phone choose **Paste invitation**, paste the complete text and choose
   **Continue** within ten minutes. With a saved invitation image, use **Scan
   invitation → Choose invitation image**. A working camera is not required.
5. Compare the phone and computer codes. If they match, choose **Codes match —
   allow this phone** on the office. If they differ, stop and create a fresh
   invitation on the intended computer.
6. Keep both apps open until the phone says **Saved on the computer. Photos
   verified.** **Allowed to connect** means office permission; it does not mean
   the phone is currently online.

## Save and submit the prepared inspection

Choose **Continue inspection** on the intended equipment/job. Answer its actual
checklist, enter the meter in the displayed unit, add notes and choose **Choose a
saved photo** to use the gallery. Leave an unknown reading blank; enter zero only
when the actual reading is zero.

**Save work** keeps a draft on the phone. **Submit for review** saves a fixed
version for office review. Both work offline. **Waiting to send** means a complete
office copy has not been confirmed. Keep the original phone; unsent work cannot
be restored to a replacement from the office alone.

Reconnect and keep both apps open. Automatic sending retries while open; **Sync
now** tries immediately. Earlier successful sending does not prove later edits
were sent, including changes to another job. Changing the selected inspection
alone does not make received work unsent. **Unsaved changes** means the current
edits are not yet saved. Save now, or go back to save before leaving.

The office reviews the submitted version. **Awaiting review** means it received
that version; complete photo bytes are checked separately before approval. Return
and approval remain attached to that exact version after corrections. Approval
records an inspection, with its findings; it does not close repairs or declare
equipment available.

## If connection fails

Reconnect both devices to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, keep both apps open and
choose **Sync now**. After changing networks, open the office connection screen,
refresh its address and create a fresh invitation. On the already enrolled phone
choose **Update computer connection** and paste it. The same office identity and
phone credential are retained; saved work is kept.

If needed, open **More connection help**. Ask the person managing the computer or
network to check local device access and TCP 47841 for this app. The firewall can
stay enabled. The app changes no firewall or VPN settings, and an error alone
does not identify which setting prevented connection.

If the computer identity cannot be verified, use an invitation from the same
office. Retain storage and keys. Same-identity recovery requires a verified private
maintenance backup and the separately saved fingerprint; see
[backup and fresh office recovery](README.md#backup-and-fresh-office-recovery).

## Physical and unassisted acceptance remain open

Automated loopback and rendered checks establish behavior and layout. A new user
still needs to try these instructions without coaching: bring invitation text or
an image to the phone, compare codes, recover from incomplete/expired text, change
hotspots and refresh/update the connection, save/reopen offline, identify queued
versus confirmed work and review the exact version. Observe where they stop and
what help they need, including at large text. Keep the firewall enabled during
connection troubleshooting.

Physical S24 gallery delivery, office approval and fresh-computer restore are
separate gates. Earlier lab note persistence/delivery does not verify this app.
